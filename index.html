How it works

Three things, in order. That’s the whole app.

No timeline. No discovery feed. No suggested people. Just what someone within 500 metres of you is offering or asking right now.

01 / DROP
Pin a thought, a plan, an invitation.
One line. «Free pizza at the office.» «Looking for a hiking partner Sunday.» «Why does floor 3 smell like pancakes at 11am?»
02 / OPEN
The pin becomes a chat. Anyone in range can join.
Your pin is its own chatroom. People nearby see it, tap in, and reply. No crowds — just the people who are actually up for it.
LenaI’m down. 19:30 at the corner pizza place?
Booked. 2 spots left.
03 / MEET
It worked out? Cool. Chat closes itself.
When the plan firms up — «see you at 7» — the chat archives. No follows, no DMs, no streaks, no notifications begging you back tomorrow.

Why it’s different

No followers. No feed. No reason to come back tomorrow.

LOKIO is for the next hour, the next block, the next person. We don’t want your daily-active minutes — we want one good real-life moment.

No global feed
If you’re not within walking distance of a pin, you don’t see it. The map ends where you can’t reach in 15 minutes.
No followers
You don’t collect people. After a chat closes, the only thing you keep is the memory of the coffee.
No DMs
Conversation lives on the pin, in front of everyone in range. No silent slide-into-DMs — if someone’s creepy, the room sees it.
Anonymous by default
Name + a gender tag is the minimum — the maximum is whatever links you choose to add later (Instagram, Telegram, nothing).
Free, works on the web
No App Store, no install, no «sign in with Apple» gate. Open app.lokio.org on any phone and you’re in.
Works everywhere
New York, Berlin, Istanbul, Tokyo — one map. Write in your language, find your people anywhere.

Is LOKIO free?
Yes. Posting, reading, joining chats, and your profile page are free forever. Paid boosts ($0.49–$4.99) amplify your reach when it matters — the base product is fully free.
Do I need to install anything?
No. LOKIO is a web app. Open app.lokio.org in your browser and you are in. No download, no app store. Native iOS and Android apps are coming soon.
