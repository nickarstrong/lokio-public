## What you pay for. What is non-refundable.

The free baseline of LOKIO is permanently free. Posting, reading, joining, and chatting are not gated by payment, in any city, on any device, forever.

### Boost — paid visibility amplification

A single paid product is offered, called **Boost**. Boost amplifies a pin's reach (geographic radius, time-to-live, priority in the map view, optional banner styling). Boost is the only paid surface in the product.

Boost is available in single purchases and a monthly bundle:

- **Boost 1 day.** From `$2` at the published base tier.
- **Boost 7 days.** From `$10` at the published base tier.
- **Boost 30 days.** From `$25` at the published base tier.
- **Boost Monthly.** From `$30/month` at the published base tier. Every pin you publish during the month is boosted for 24 hours. No automatic re-billing — each month is a separate, deliberate purchase.

### City tier ramp

The prices above are the **base tier**. Each city has a tier multiplier that ramps with platform activity in that city. The ramp starts after a free **SEED_FLOOR** threshold of approximately 500 active users in the city, after which prices begin to grow gradually with density. Early users in low-density cities pay close to zero; the price approaches the full base tier only as the city scales.

The current charged price for your city is displayed before purchase confirmation. By confirming the purchase, you accept that price.

### Billing mechanics

- Payment is processed by Apple Pay or Google Pay. Your card data is never seen or stored by LOKIO.
- Each purchase is a single, discrete transaction. We do not run automatic subscription renewal. Boost Monthly does not auto-renew.
- Boost delivers visibility. **Once a Boost has been delivered (the pin has been published with the paid amplification active), it is non-refundable.**
- If a payment fails or is reversed by the platform, the corresponding amplification will be removed.
- Fraudulent purchases or chargebacks may result in immediate account suspension.

Tax (VAT, GST, sales tax) is charged where required by your jurisdiction and is included in or added to the published price as applicable. Prices are quoted in USD.
