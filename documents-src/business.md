## If you reach customers in walking distance, this is for you.

LOKIO is geo-bounded by design. A banner pin appears to people who open the map in your district. Not the city, not the country, not somebody scrolling at home in another country. The people who can walk to you in the next hour.{.muted}

**Café.** "First coffee free, anyone in the district right now." Five people walk in within the hour. Three return next week.

**Trainer.** Solo yoga or kickboxing instructor fills tonight's class with one map post. No subscription platform fee.

**Market.** Neighbourhood market lets the street know they have fresh fish today. No marketer required. The street walks in.

**Street vendor.** Announces today's location to passers-by within walking distance. The pin updates with the cart.

A small café spends roughly **$3,500** per month on marketing in the existing model:

On LOKIO, the same café publishes a banner pin for **$4.99**. The pin is visible to every user who opens the map in the district. The free-coffee filter surfaces it on top of regular pins to people actively looking for offers right now. ROI is measurable on the same day.

::: section id=tiers num="05 · Pricing"

## Free in a new city. Full price when the city is busy.

A new city has zero supply. Charging $4.99 on day one would kill adoption. So prices ramp with city density. The coefficient is automatic, not set by a human. The ramp is one-way: once a city moves up, prices do not come back down.

| Tier | When | Coef. | Banner | Slot | Visibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0 · Seed** | New city, no critical mass | × 0 | Free | Free | Free |
| **1 · Early** | First active publishers | × 0.3 | $1.49 | $0.15 | $0.59 |
| **2 · Growing** | District-level density | × 0.6 | $2.99 | $0.29 | $1.19 |
| **3 · Active** | City has crossed threshold | × 1.0 | $4.99 | $0.49 | $1.99 |

If your city is at tier 0, your banner pin costs **nothing**. The product earns from your district only when your district is busy enough to deliver real customers. **The same idea Uber discovered through driver subsidies.** Automated.{.muted}

A banner pin on LOKIO appears to a person who **opened the map looking for something to do near them.** Their attention is already on the map. The free-coffee filter exists because a meaningful share of users tap it. The banner pin is what they came looking for.
