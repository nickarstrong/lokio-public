## What this is NOT

**Not a subscription.** You do not pay monthly. You pay per pin, when you want, if you want. $0 in new cities.

**Not advertising.** Your pin does not interrupt anyone. It appears to people who opened the map looking for something nearby. You are not buying attention — you are answering a question.

**Not complicated.** There is no dashboard. No campaign manager. No analytics suite. You write a sentence, pick banner, and publish. Five seconds.

**Not a marketplace.** You do not list your business in a directory. You post an offer — it lives for a few hours, a day, or up to 30 days with a boost. When it expires, it disappears cleanly. No stale listings from six months ago. Every post is fresh.

**Not Instagram.** You do not need followers, a content strategy, or a photographer. You need one sentence about what is happening at your place right now.

**Not exclusive.** One table or fifty locations — the mechanic is the same. A single café posts one pin. A chain posts one pin per location. Each pin works locally, reaches the people nearby, and costs the same $4.99. No enterprise tier, no bulk discount, no special treatment. Everyone plays by the same rules on the map.
