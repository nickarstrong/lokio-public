01 · Who this is for

If you reach customers in walking distance, this is for you.

LOKIO is geo-bounded by design. A banner pin appears to people who open the map in your district. Not the city, not the country, not somebody scrolling at home in another country. The people who can walk to you in the next hour.

Café

"First coffee free, anyone in the district right now." Five people walk in within the hour. Three return next week.

Trainer

Solo yoga or kickboxing instructor fills tonight's class with one map post. No subscription platform fee.

Market

Neighbourhood market lets the street know they have fresh fish today. No marketer required. The street walks in.

Street vendor

Announces today's location to passers-by within walking distance. The pin updates with the cart.

Not for chains. Chains do not need geo-bounded posts because they own broadcast advertising. LOKIO mechanically does not scale for them. By design.

05 · Pricing

Free in a new city. Full price when the city is busy.

A new city has zero supply. Charging $4.99 on day one would kill adoption. So prices ramp with city density. The coefficient is automatic, not set by a human. The ramp is one-way: once a city moves up, prices do not come back down.

06 · Why this works

Not advertising people hate. Invitations they wait for.

Display ads interrupt. Banner blindness is real. People scroll past. The conversion math has been getting worse for a decade.

A banner pin on LOKIO appears to a person who opened the map looking for something to do near them. Their attention is already on the map. The free-coffee filter exists because a meaningful share of users tap it. The banner pin is what they came looking for.

Same channel. Different intent. A Google Ads click is interruption. A LOKIO banner pin is the answer to a question the user just asked.

The mechanic is the same one that made WeChat Pay work for street vendors in China and Pinduoduo work for budget shoppers. Small business reaches its customer directly without an intermediary. The ads model takes a cut between merchant and customer. LOKIO removes the cut.
